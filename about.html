Our Sweet Story

Empowering bakers to turn their imagination into delicious reality through premium quality tools.

Born from a Passion for Baking

Founded in 2020 by a team of professional pastry chefs, CakeTools was born out of frustration with the lack of accessible, high-quality cake decorating tools on the market.

We realized that whether you're a seasoned professional running a bakery or an enthusiastic home baker crafting weekend treats, you need reliable tools that won't let you down.

Today, we're proud to serve over 12,000 bakers worldwide, providing them with the professional-grade equipment they need to create show-stopping cakes.
